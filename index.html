ENGLISH SUMMARY

About Okemon Company

Okemon Company (trade name: Watashi Torisetsu) is a sole proprietorship founded in 2024 in Kobe, Japan, by Okemon (Kenjiro Mimata), who worked for 16 years in elder care and disability services as a care worker, service manager and facility director.

What we do:

  • Strengths coaching: reading your CliftonStrengths results in your own words, with a personalized "user manual" made with Claude Code after each session.
  • AI adoption for care and healthcare workplaces: a paid six-session AI training program for a healthcare workplace (started September 2026), and a care-record drafting demo on the Claude API (synthetic data only, in progress).
  • Motion graphics: Motion Recipe, an app that turns one line of plain Japanese into a Claude Code prompt, with a gallery of example motion graphics.
